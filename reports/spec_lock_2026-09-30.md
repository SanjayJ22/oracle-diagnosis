## 3. Selection rules fixed before looking at any feature value

1. Attack candidates come from the 24-incident table plus the A2 provisional labels; an attack
   enters only if two independent sources confirm `S`, the consumer and the mechanism (recorded in
   the pilot report).
2. An attack whose `S` never fires the trigger under G1 is a **trigger miss**: listed in the
   report, not in the analysis class, and not dropped silently.
3. Benign episodes: enumerate all trigger firings for the named events and sources, then, where a
   cell (event x source) has more than 6 episodes, draw 6 with a seeded RNG (seed 20260930).
   Selection never looks at any feature value.
4. `T` for benign episodes is the first firing block, refined to the exact block by per-block reads.
5. No benign episode within 24 hours (7,200 blocks) of an exploit on the same source (checked
   against the 165-incident table, the pilot attacks, and any exploit found during source checks).
6. `R` hierarchy per A1 s4; whichever tier is used is recorded, and the same tier must be
   available for both classes within a source type.
