## Methodology notes

- **Threshold**: 3% over 24h -- lowered from phase 2's 5% specifically to reach a larger target on top of the 85 already locked, for phase 3's 45->105->~200 total scaling goal. This means phase 3's volatility incidents are, on average, smaller and calmer moves than the original 12, phase 1's 18 (10%+), and phase 2's 55 (5%+). Flagged explicitly since this affects the deterministic baseline's reserve_skew-threshold behavior in a known, already-documented way.
- **No lookahead**: heartbeat_elapsed is read at block-1, matching this project's fixed anchoring-artifact convention.
- **Cleanliness gate**: unchanged from phases 1-2 -- 5% cross-venue agreement, heartbeat_elapsed <= 1800s.
- **Block resolution**: timestamp-linear estimate + short local binary search, same as phases 1-2.
- Every value in the table above is a live on-chain or exchange-API read for this run, not estimated or interpolated.
